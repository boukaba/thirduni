# Week 4 — Product Thinking, Pitching & Security

## What I learned

### Storytelling (Victor)
Word farming framework: soil, seed, water, sunlight, fertilizer. Applied to Pullrun — the why behind the runtime, not the features.

### The Pitch (Daniel)
10-slide skeleton, one idea per slide. Four rooms: hackathon (roadmap), accelerator (gaps), angel (money + plan), VC (proof + warm intros). Recording script synced per slide, 2:50.

### Think Like a Product Architect (Souheila)
Start with the problem, not the technology. Four questions: who, what, context, value. Three problems exercise — no solutions, just problems.

### Food Waste in Algeria (Souheila)
The competitor is often a spreadsheet someone refuses to replace, not another startup. When two research numbers disagree, understanding why is the first lesson in research.

### Zero to Verified (Joe / NEAR DevHub)
TEE: a private room the data goes into that nobody can peek in. Attestation proves where the work happened. Per-response signatures prove what went in and came out. Spec sheet before build. Spending limits on every API key.

### Backend Security
- **STRIDE** — design-time threat modeling. Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Information disclosure, Denial of service, Elevation of privilege.
- **OWASP Top 10** — runtime vulnerability recognition. Broken access control, cryptographic failures, injection, insecure design, security misconfiguration, and more.
- **Bug triage** — identify, match, patch. Name the problem, OWASP points to the fix.
- **The mindset** — think like an attacker, design like a defender. STRIDE predicts, OWASP diagnoses.
